# MirrorMe Feature Ideas - Comprehensive Looksmaxxing Categories

Based on research into looksmaxxing and self-improvement techniques, here are feature ideas organized by category:

## 🎯 **Core Facial Analysis Features**

### 1. **Facial Symmetry Analysis** (Enhanced)
- **Current**: Basic symmetry score
- **Enhancements**:
  - Golden ratio analysis (phi ratio measurements)
  - Eye symmetry (width, height, position)
  - Nose symmetry (deviation from center line)
  - Lip symmetry and fullness analysis
  - Cheekbone prominence and symmetry
  - Jawline symmetry (left vs right)
  - Ear alignment
  - Detailed asymmetry heatmap overlay

### 2. **Jawline & Facial Structure**
- **Jawline Definition Score**:
  - Analysis of jaw angle (ideal ~120-130°)
  - Chin projection measurement
  - Lower third of face proportion
  - Jaw width vs face width ratio
  - Mandible angle visualization
- **Mewing Progress Tracker**:
  - Daily tongue posture reminders
  - Before/after comparisons
  - Progress timeline with photos
  - Mewing technique guide with video
- **Cheekbone Analysis**:
  - Prominence scoring
  - High vs low cheekbone placement
  - Symmetry measurement
  - Hollowing/volume assessment

### 3. **Eye Area Analysis**
- **Eye Shape & Symmetry**:
  - Eye width, height, and spacing
  - Canthal tilt (positive, neutral, negative)
  - Eye symmetry score
  - Upper eyelid exposure
  - Under-eye area analysis
- **Eyebrow Analysis**:
  - Shape recommendations based on face shape
  - Thickness analysis
  - Arch position
  - Spacing recommendations
  - Grooming suggestions

### 4. **Nose Analysis**
- **Nose Harmony**:
  - Width-to-height ratio
  - Bridge definition
  - Tip projection
  - Nostril width analysis
  - Nasal symmetry
  - Profile analysis (side view)

### 5. **Lips & Mouth Area**
- **Lip Analysis**:
  - Upper/lower lip ratio
  - Cupid's bow definition
  - Lip fullness score
  - Corner lift (smile analysis)
  - Philtrum length measurement

### 8. **Skin Progress Timeline**
